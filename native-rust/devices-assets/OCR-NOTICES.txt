Buddy includes unmodified Ocrs Latin text detection and recognition models by Robert Knight.
Model source: https://github.com/robertknight/ocrs-models
Model files: https://ocrs-models.s3-accelerate.amazonaws.com/text-detection.onnx
             https://ocrs-models.s3-accelerate.amazonaws.com/text-recognition.onnx
The models were trained on the HierText dataset and are shared under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International.
